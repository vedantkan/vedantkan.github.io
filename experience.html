Columbia University

Junior Data Scientist · Department of Neurology

2023 – 2026 · New York, NY

I spent three years in Dr. Jan Claassen's Disorders of Consciousness (DoC) Lab, which studies cognitive motor dissociation: patients who look unresponsive after a brain injury but whose EEG shows they can follow commands. My work sat between the bedside and the analysis, turning continuous EEG and clinical data into reliable datasets and building the statistical tools the group used to detect hidden consciousness and predict recovery.

  • Detecting hidden consciousness. Developed and refined the EEG pipeline behind the group's CMD and brain–computer interface studies: event detection, spectral features, support-vector-machine classification and permutation testing.
  • Modelling recovery. Analysed long-term outcomes after brain injury with survival and time-to-event models, mixed-effects and logistic regression, and prediction-model evaluation (discrimination, calibration, decision curves) for work published in Brain, Nature Medicine, Neurocritical Care and Critical Care.
  • Making the data usable. Built reproducible Python, R, SQL and Azure pipelines for terabyte-scale EEG, physiological and health-record data, and wrote R packages for automated quality control and statistical reporting.
  • Taking testing beyond one hospital. Designed and validated a smartphone- and web-based workflow for running the EEG command paradigm across sites, with encoded audio triggers, signal acquisition and secure cloud transfer.
